---
name: Competency versus authorization
description: Separation between professional competency evidence and operational ERP permissions for estimator and QS workflows.
---

Store SKKNI/SKKK scheme, level, certificate reference, validity, and verification as a professional profile. Never derive ERP roles, approval rights, or project access automatically from that profile.

**Why:** A competency standard describes demonstrated professional capability, while authorization is an organizational control. Combining them would let a profile change bypass project assignment and segregation of duties.

**How to apply:** Keep operational roles and permissions explicit and project-scoped. Treat competency records as reference evidence, show their source and verification status, and require an independent authorized reviewer for final RAB approval.
